# Studio Grand Piano attribution

EarLab's bundled Studio Grand Piano is derived from **Salamander Grand Piano v3**
by **Alexander Holm**. Source recordings and source package are available at
https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano.

The source work is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported
license: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/.

EarLab includes a reduced training-register subset converted to mono 48 kHz Opus
for efficient browser playback. No endorsement by the original author is implied.
